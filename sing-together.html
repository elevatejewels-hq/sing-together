This starter library is deliberately small, and every song in it is a real public-domain or traditional melody written out as note data. The full library will be built from a link or source the owner supplies, holding most of the songs. Each song gets converted the same lightweight way — title, style tag, tempo, key, melody as a note sequence, and its harmony pattern, with no audio files at all — then the library is downloaded once and cached on the device, so even the full library keeps working offline. How it works

Three steps, all inside this one page.

  1. You singAny song, any key — hum it, belt it, or sing it softly in the car.
  2. It understands the notesYour pitch is measured many times a second, named as a note, and read for style: range, pace, and how long you hold each note.
  3. A second voice joins inThe partner voice follows your melody a harmony part away, in the style you chose — or the style it detected in your song.
Private by design: listening, note detection and the partner voice all run locally in your own browser. Nothing you sing is recorded, uploaded, or visible to anyone else — every visitor's session is theirs alone. The partner in this version is a synthesised singing voice generated live on your device.
