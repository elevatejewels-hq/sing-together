This starter library is deliberately small, and every song in it is a real public-domain or traditional melody written out as note data. The full library will be built from a link or source the owner supplies, holding most of the songs. Each song gets converted the same lightweight way — title, style tag, tempo, key, melody as a note sequence, and its chord pattern, with no audio files at all — then the library is downloaded once and cached on the device, so even the full library keeps working offline. How it works

Three steps, all inside this one page.

  1. You singAny song, any key — hum it, belt it, or sing it softly in the car.
  2. It understands the notesYour pitch is measured many times a second, named as a note, and read for style: range, pace, and how long you hold each note.
  3. The band joins the songWhen a library song is recognised, the grand piano opens with its melody and chords over a steady beat — and the bass guitar comes in the second time around. No song recognised, no noise: the instruments stay silent.
Private by design: listening, note detection and the piano & bass accompaniment all run locally in your own browser. Nothing you sing is recorded, uploaded, or visible to anyone else — every visitor's session is theirs alone. The grand piano and bass guitar are synthesised live on your device — no recordings, no AI.
